Data Deletion

Last updated: May 13, 2026

ReplyTrove respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of your data, including data collected through Instagram and Facebook integrations.

How to Delete Your ReplyTrove Account and Data

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by either:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Email: Send a request to privacy@replytrove.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account.

Upon receiving your request, we will:

  • Verify your identity
  • Delete your account and all personal data within 30 days
  • Send you a confirmation email once deletion is complete

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (email, business name, settings)
  • All orders, customers, products, and expenses you created
  • All tasks and notes
  • Uploaded photos and receipts
  • Connected channel credentials (Instagram, WhatsApp)
  • Push notification tokens

What We May Retain

We may retain certain data where required by law:

  • Financial transaction records for up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics that cannot identify you
  • Records of deletion requests for compliance purposes

Facebook / Instagram Data Deletion

If you connected your Facebook or Instagram account to ReplyTrove, you can also revoke access directly through Meta:

  • Go to your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Find ReplyTrove and click Remove
  • This will revoke our access to your Instagram/Facebook data immediately

After revoking access on Meta, please also submit a deletion request to us at privacy@replytrove.app to ensure all data stored in our systems is also removed.

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion, contact us at privacy@replytrove.app. We will respond within 5 business days.
